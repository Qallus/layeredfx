# Assets and content boundaries

The included room, kitchen, glass, and paint scenes are original editable SVG illustrations made for this homepage prototype. They are deliberately marked as concepts. The original/finish comparison uses two variants of the same conceptual room; it is not an actual renovation record.

The draft mark is a small geometric layer symbol paired with a text wordmark. Replace it with Jeremy's approved LayeredFX logo when supplied.

No stock-photo license, photographer credit, or completed-client claim is implied by these illustrations. Do not describe them as genuine LayeredFX project photos.

Before launch, provide and approve:

- Official LayeredFX logos and brand direction.
- Real, authorized project photos and before/after pairs.
- Public contact email/phone, service areas, and business details.
- Actual material manufacturers, collections, and product references.
- Approved service language and any warranties, qualifications, or technical claims.
- Privacy and terms content appropriate to the real data flows.

No invented testimonials, years-in-business counts, project totals, licensing numbers, starting prices, or guaranteed timelines are included.

The four finish labels are inspiration only. They should not become purchasable products, measured color references, or compatibility claims without verified material data.

`social-card.png` is generated from the supplied `social-card.svg`. The PNG is used for social preview metadata because SVG support is not dependable across social crawlers. Review the image and metadata on the target platforms before launch.
